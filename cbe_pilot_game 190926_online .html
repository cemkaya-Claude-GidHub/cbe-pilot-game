ⓘ HOW THIS BONUS WORKS — tap to read
A month qualifies the moment you either beat last month by +1% or hold Elite Tier that month — same "met" flag the CBE Index table already uses, nothing new to track. In a qualifying month you get two things: 10% of the real euros you saved (from the Cost Ledger, uncapped — the more you genuinely save, the more you earn, always), plus a floor bonus — up to 10% of that month's salary — that rewards the parts of CBE that don't show up in fuel numbers at all. The floor never pushes the total past what was actually saved: in a quiet month it shrinks to fit, so the company can never pay out more than it saved. In a non-qualifying month, only the 10%-of-savings piece applies — no floor.

Flying with other pilots? A saving on a shared flight was made by the whole crew together, not one person alone — so crew size (② above) divides the 10%-of-savings piece evenly between everyone flying that leg (2 pilots → 5% each, 3 → 3.3% each, and so on), so the company's total payout for that saving always stays at 10%, never more, no matter how many pilots were on board. The floor bonus is untouched by this — it's tied to your own salary and your own performance, not shared.
Real savings are summed from your saved Cost Ledger days (Statistic · Money Saving) that fall within the selected month. Save each flying day there first, or this will show €0.
